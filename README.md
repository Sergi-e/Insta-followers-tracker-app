# Followers Tracker

A privacy-first Instagram follower analysis tool. See who doesn't follow you back, recent unfollowers, and pending follow requests, entirely in your browser. Your data never leaves your device.

## Live Demo

[Open Followers Tracker](https://insta-followers-tracker-app-three.vercel.app/)

## How it works

Upload your Instagram JSON export files, and the app analyzes them locally. Nothing is sent to any server.

**Required files from your Instagram export:**

- `followers_1.json`
- `following.json`
- `recently_unfollowed_profiles.json`
- `recent_follow_requests.json`

## Tech

Built with plain HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks, build tools, or external dependencies.

## Deploy

This is a static single-file app that deploys to Vercel automatically on every push to `main`.

[Deploy with Vercel](https://vercel.com/new/clone?repository-url=https://github.com/Sergi-e/Insta-followers-tracker-app)
